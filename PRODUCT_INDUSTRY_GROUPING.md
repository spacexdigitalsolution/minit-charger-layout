Based on the Applications table and Product Overview in the Product Comparison Matrix, cross-referenced with the four industries listed in your site's nav (Aviation, Warehouse/Manufacturing, Low Speed Vehicles, Vehicle/Driver Tracking), here's how the products map as parent (primary/lead) vs. child (supporting/compatible) products for each:
1. Aviation (Airport GSE)
Parent product: Altus II — described on the homepage as the outdoor dual-port charger with EV option; the matrix lists its typical customers as Airports, Airlines, Ground Handlers, and its "Best For" as standard airport GSE fleets.
Child products:
Magnus — also serves airports/fleet operators and is the only product with SCADA/ERP integration specifically noted for Airport Systems.
Mobilus — serves airports/utilities via mobile/temporary charging.
CellTrac — onboard BMS/GPS layer for tracking GSE equipment.
AssetPro 360 (Cumulus) — fleet-wide monitoring and analytics layered on top of any airport charger.
2. Warehouse/Manufacturing (Material Handling / Indoor)
Parent product: Maximus — homepage describes it as the "high-capacity indoor charger with dual ports for large-scale use"; matrix confirms its use case as large warehouses/distribution centers and multi-shift industrial operations.
Child products:
Momentus — the mid-level, compact indoor forklift/MHE charger (homepage: "mid-level charger for material handling, fast installation, and power").
CellTrac — battery/asset tracking for MHE fleets.
AssetPro 360 (Cumulus)— analytics and reporting layer.
3. Low Speed Vehicles
Parent product: Durus — homepage: "compact charger ideal for low-speed vehicles and mobility"; matrix lists typical customers as golf courses, AGVs, and utility fleets, with built-in BMS.
Child products:
CellTrac — GPS/SoC tracking (though Durus already has built-in BMS/tracking).
AssetPro 360 (Cumulus) — fleet-level reporting for LSV fleets.
4. Vehicle/Driver Tracking
Parent product: CellTrac — homepage: "Onboard BMS with GPS, SoC monitoring, and cloud connection" — this is the dedicated tracking product.
Child products:
AssetPro 360 (Cumulus) — the fleet management dashboard that consumes CellTrac's data for real-time analytics and alerts.
Mobilus — the only charger in the matrix with GPS/Asset Tracking marked natively (✓) outside of CellTrac.
