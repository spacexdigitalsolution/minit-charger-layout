In short

What is an electric GSE fast charger?

An electric GSE (ground support equipment) fast charger is a DC charging system built to power airside vehicles — baggage tractors, belt loaders, tow tractors, and pushback tugs — faster than standard AC charging, while withstanding outdoor ramp conditions. Minit Charger's Altus II is a dual-port, outdoor-rated (IP65) fast charger purpose-built for this environment, supporting lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery chemistries from a single unit.

Frequently asked questions

Industrial charging questions airport buyers ask.

What is an eGSE fast charger?
An eGSE (electric ground support equipment) fast charger is a DC charging system built to power airside equipment like baggage tractors, belt loaders, tow tractors, and pushback tugs faster than standard AC charging, while withstanding outdoor ramp conditions.
How fast can an eGSE charger like the Altus II charge airport ground support equipment?
Altus II delivers 30–80 kW of DC fast charging across two ports simultaneously. Actual charge time depends on the battery chemistry, pack size, and state of charge of the vehicle being charged — your assessment call will map this to your specific fleet.
Do airport GSE chargers need to be rated for outdoor installation?
Yes. Ramp-based chargers are exposed to rain, extreme heat, cold, and constant vibration from nearby aircraft and vehicle traffic. Altus II carries an outdoor-rated IP65 enclosure, built for ramp duty rather than adapted from an indoor warehouse design.
Can one charger support both lead-acid and lithium GSE batteries?
Yes. Altus II supports lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery chemistries from the same dual-port unit, so a mixed fleet or a mid-refresh chemistry change doesn't require a different charger.
What is Bridge Power Manager (BPM) and how does it help airport charging projects?
BPM is Minit Charger's intelligent power-sharing system. It allocates available power across every charger on a shared electrical feed in real time, which can let airports add charging stations without a separate utility infrastructure upgrade — a common reason GSE electrification projects stall.
How do I track battery health and location across a GSE fleet remotely?
CellTrac monitors battery state of charge, health, and GPS location on each vehicle, feeding that data into the AssetPro 360 / Cumulus dashboard so operations and maintenance teams can see the whole fleet from one screen.
How long does it take to install eGSE fast chargers at an airport?
Timelines depend on site conditions, charger count, and whether your existing power feed can support them with BPM or needs additional capacity. A site survey during your charging assessment scopes an accurate schedule for your ramp.
What connector types work with Minit Charger's GSE chargers?
Altus II supports Anderson, Euro, and REMA connectors, with multiple connector configurations available to match the tugs, tractors, and belt loaders already in your fleet.
