Before the survey

The charger is never the problem. Until it is.

Nobody buys a charger because they want a charger. They buy one because something else in the operation keeps breaking.

"We electrified the fleet, then found out we can't charge it fast enough."
Altus II is DC fast charging, not overnight AC. Two ports on one pedestal means opportunity charging instead of a queue.
"Half our equipment is lead acid, half is going lithium, and nobody wants to buy twice."
One unit covers lead acid, lithium and EV, so a mixed fleet doesn't need a mixed inventory.
"The utility quoted us a service upgrade before we could add a single charger."
Altus II runs on a single power feed, and Bridge Power Manager can share available power — often more charge points without new service.
Beyond the charger

You can't schedule around a battery you can't see.

Cumulus brings every charger and every session into one view. CellTrac reports battery health and location from the equipment itself, so a failing pack shows up before it strands a tug at a gate.

Cumulus — fleet management platform CellTrac — on-vehicle battery monitoring
