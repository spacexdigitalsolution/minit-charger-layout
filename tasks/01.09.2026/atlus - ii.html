What is the Minit Charger Altus II?

Altus II is an outdoor-rated, dual-port DC fast charger built for airport ground support equipment (GSE) — the everyday charger for tugs, belt loaders, and baggage tractors across a standard airport fleet. It delivers 30–80 kW across two ports, supports lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery chemistries with Anderson, Euro, and REMA connectors, and connects to AssetPro 360 / Cumulus for fleet-wide charge and battery data.

Built for ramp reality

Where an everyday GSE charger actually earns its keep.

A charger that only performs well on a spec sheet doesn't help a ground crew at 5am. Here's where that gap shows up, and how Altus II is specified against it.

Failure mode

Charger not built for the ramp

Most industrial chargers are adapted from warehouse designs, then asked to survive rain, heat, and constant vibration on an active ramp.

Design response

Altus II is outdoor-rated from the ground up, built specifically for high-cycle GSE duty — not retrofitted for it.

Failure mode

One battery type, one charger

A fleet with lead-acid tugs and a newer lithium belt loader often ends up needing two different chargers just to keep both running.

Design response

Altus II charges lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery packs from the same dual-port unit.

Failure mode

Downtime nobody sees coming

Without remote diagnostics, the first sign of a charger fault is usually a dead tug at the gate, not a dashboard alert.

Design response

Cloud connectivity, remote monitoring, and predictive maintenance alerts flag a fault before it strands a vehicle.

Failure mode

Power-constrained ramps stall expansion

Adding chargers to an older ramp often runs into the same wall: the existing feed wasn't sized for more fast chargers.

Design response

Altus II pairs with Bridge Power Manager (BPM) for constrained infrastructure, sharing available power across chargers on a single feed.

Failure mode

No accountability for shared ramp power

Multiple airlines or ground handlers sharing the same charging infrastructure need a way to see who used what — not a shared utility line nobody can reconcile.

Design response

AssetPro 360 integration gives usage and charge-history data by vehicle and session, supporting cost allocation across airlines and handlers.

Failure mode

A charging cable left on the ramp

A cable dropped and forgotten after charging doesn't just look untidy — it's a tripping hazard, and repeated run-overs from tugs and belt loaders wear through the jacket until it fails or shorts.

Design response

Altus II retracts its charging cable automatically into the housing the moment a session ends — nobody has to remember to coil it, and it isn't lying on the ramp for the next vehicle to run over.

Frequently asked questions

What buyers ask about Altus II.

What is the Minit Charger Altus II?
Altus II is an outdoor-rated, dual-port DC fast charger built for airport ground support equipment (GSE) — the everyday charger for tugs, belt loaders, and baggage tractors across a standard airport fleet.
How much power does Altus II deliver?
Altus II delivers 30–80 kW of DC fast charging across two ports. Actual output depends on configuration — a charging assessment will confirm the right setup for your fleet.
What connectors does Altus II support?
Altus II supports Anderson, Euro, and REMA connectors, matching the tugs, tractors, and belt loaders already in use across most airport GSE fleets.
Can Altus II charge lithium and lead-acid batteries from the same unit?
Yes. Altus II supports lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery chemistries from the same dual-port charger.
Is Altus II rated for outdoor installation?
Yes. Altus II is outdoor-rated and supports both indoor and outdoor pedestal installation, built for ramp conditions rather than adapted from an indoor design.
Does Altus II integrate with existing airport systems?
Yes. Altus II connects via cellular, Wi-Fi, or Ethernet and integrates with airport systems, plus CellTrac and AssetPro 360 / Cumulus for fleet-wide charge and battery data.
Can Altus II add power sharing for a constrained electrical feed?
Yes. Altus II can be paired with Bridge Power Manager (BPM), Minit Charger's intelligent power-sharing system, to allocate available power across multiple chargers on a single feed — useful where ramp electrical capacity is limited.
How is charging usage tracked when multiple airlines or ground handlers share a charger?
AssetPro 360 / Cumulus records charge history and usage data by vehicle and session, which supports cost allocation across airlines and ground handlers sharing the same charging infrastructure.
Does the Altus II charging cable retract automatically?
Yes, as an optional cable management feature. The charging cable retracts automatically into the housing at the end of a session, so ground crews don't need to manually coil it and it isn't left lying on the ramp where it could be a tripping hazard or run over by other vehicles.
