In short

What is a low speed vehicle (LSV) charger?

A low speed vehicle (LSV) charger powers small electric vehicles such as golf carts, campus shuttles, security carts, and municipal utility vehicles. Unlike a pedestal or wall charger, Minit Charger's Durus is an onboard charger installed on the vehicle itself — it plugs into any compatible standard outlet and supports lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery chemistries, so fleets don't need to build dedicated charging infrastructure at every parking or storage location.

The cost of outgrowing your charging plan

LSV fleets grow faster than charging infrastructure keeps up.

A resort, campus, or municipal fleet rarely stays the same size two seasons in a row — and a charging plan built for last year's fleet becomes this year's bottleneck.

Pain point

Fleet growth outpaces charging infrastructure

A property adds five more carts for the season, and suddenly there aren't enough charging bays, outlets, or staff hours to keep every vehicle topped off overnight.

Minit response

Durus's onboard charger travels with the vehicle — plug into any compatible outlet at any bay, cart barn, or maintenance closet. No dedicated charging station buildout per new vehicle.

Pain point

Mixed-brand fleets, mismatched chargers

A fleet built up over years ends up with carts and utility vehicles from different manufacturers side by side — each with a different charger, a different plug, a different failure mode.

Minit response

Durus supports lead-acid, lithium, and EV chemistries with SB350, Euro, or open-lead connector options, so it standardizes charging across a genuinely mixed fleet.

Pain point

No visibility until a vehicle dies mid-shift

A cart that won't hold a charge doesn't announce itself until a guest is stranded on the back nine or a groundskeeper is stuck at the far end of the property.

Minit response

Durus's built-in battery management and CellTrac integration track battery health and charge status in real time, flagging a failing pack before it strands a vehicle.

Pain point

Outdoor duty cycles most chargers weren't built for

Golf carts and utility vehicles live outside through heat, rain, and an off-season of sitting idle — conditions that shorten the life of a charger built for a warehouse floor.

Minit response

Durus is rated from -25°C to 60°C and installs indoors or outdoors, holding up to the same duty cycle as the vehicle it's mounted on.

Frequently asked questions

Charging questions LSV fleet buyers ask.

What types of low-speed vehicles are compatible with Minit Charger?
Durus is built for golf carts, utility vehicles, AGVs, and other small electric equipment, supporting lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery chemistries so it can charge a mixed-brand LSV fleet.
Can Minit Charger be used in outdoor environments?
Yes. Durus is rated from -25°C to 60°C and supports both indoor and outdoor installation, matching the duty cycle of golf carts and utility vehicles that live outside.
How does cloud connectivity enhance my operations?
Durus connects to AssetPro 360 / Cumulus for remote monitoring, charge history, and predictive maintenance alerts, and integrates with CellTrac for battery health and asset tracking, so a fleet manager can see every vehicle's charge status from one dashboard instead of walking the cart barn.
What is a low speed vehicle (LSV) charger?
An LSV charger powers small electric vehicles such as golf carts, campus shuttles, and municipal utility carts. Unlike a pedestal or wall charger, Durus is an onboard charger installed on the vehicle itself, so it travels with the vehicle rather than requiring a dedicated charging station.
What's the difference between onboard and offboard charging?
An offboard or pedestal charger is a fixed station the vehicle must return to. An onboard charger like Durus is built into the vehicle itself and plugs into any compatible standard outlet, so charging infrastructure doesn't need to be built out at every parking or storage location.
Does Durus support lithium and lead-acid batteries?
Yes. Durus supports lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery chemistries with SB350, Euro, or open-lead connector options, so a fleet with mixed battery types or brands can standardize on one charger.
Can Durus charging infrastructure qualify for grant funding?
Many state and local clean-fleet programs offer incentives for electrifying golf, resort, campus, and municipal vehicle fleets. Eligibility depends on your location and program — the Grant Eligibility Guide download above walks through what to check first. [Client to confirm current program references before publishing.]
What venues use Minit Charger for LSV fleets?
Golf courses and resorts, university and corporate campuses, municipal and utility fleets, and airport landside operations commonly use Minit Charger's onboard LSV charging for golf carts and utility vehicles.
