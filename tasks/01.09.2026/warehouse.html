In short

What is a fast charger for forklifts and material handling equipment?

A material handling equipment (MHE) fast charger is a DC charging system built to power forklifts, pallet jacks, order pickers, reach trucks, and AGVs faster than standard AC charging, so equipment can top off during breaks and shift changes instead of requiring a full battery swap. Minit Charger's Momentus (20 kW, single port) and Maximus (40 kW, dual port) are compact, wall- or pedestal-mounted chargers built for exactly this — supporting lead-acid, lithium, EV, and AGM battery chemistries from the same platform.

The cost of the battery-swap routine

Multi-shift floors don't have room for downtime that's optional.

A forklift parked for a battery swap isn't broken — it's just not moving product. Multiply that across a fleet and three shifts, and the swap routine becomes its own hidden labor line.

Pain point

Battery swapping eats labor hours every shift

An operator pulled off the floor to swap a battery isn't picking, staging, or loading — and a spare-battery room adds its own labor, space, and safety overhead most warehouses would rather not manage.

Minit response

Momentus and Maximus deliver fast, opportunity-style charging between shifts and breaks, so a full swap program isn't the only way to keep a multi-shift fleet running.

Pain point

Mixed equipment, mixed batteries, one charger that can't keep up

Lead-acid forklifts, an AGM order picker, a lithium pilot on the newest reach trucks — most charging fleets end up with a different charger for every battery type on the floor.

Minit response

Momentus and Maximus support lead-acid, lithium, EV, and AGM chemistries from the same platform, with interchangeable connectors so procurement isn't locked into one equipment brand.

Pain point

Every charger eats floor space you'd rather use for racking

Traditional charger cabinets weren't designed for a warehouse floor plan — they're bulky, they take up aisle space, and they don't move easily when the layout changes.

Minit response

Modern SiC power electronics keep Momentus and Maximus compact and wall- or pedestal-mountable, indoors or outdoors, so charging fits the floor plan instead of dictating it.

Frequently asked questions

Industrial charging questions warehouse buyers ask.

What types of batteries are compatible with Minit Charger?
Momentus and Maximus support lead-acid, lithium, EV, and AGM battery chemistries from the same charger, so a mixed-chemistry warehouse fleet doesn't need a different charger for every battery type.
How does the cloud-connected system benefit my operations?
Every charger connects to AssetPro 360 / Cumulus for remote monitoring, charge history and analytics, and predictive maintenance alerts, so operations and maintenance teams can see fleet-wide charging and battery health from one dashboard instead of checking each charger individually.
Can Minit Charger handle a mixed fleet?
Yes. Momentus and Maximus are chemistry-agnostic and support multiple connector options (SB350, Euro, REMA), so forklifts, pallet jacks, reach trucks, and AGVs from different manufacturers can charge from the same platform.
What is the difference between Momentus and Maximus?
Momentus is a compact 20 kW single-port charger suited to indoor forklift operations. Maximus is a 40 kW dual-port charger built for high-throughput, multi-shift industrial operations that need two vehicles charging at once.
What is opportunity charging and does it replace battery swapping?
Opportunity charging means topping off a battery during breaks, shift changes, or idle periods instead of fully depleting it and swapping to a spare. Fast, compact chargers like Momentus and Maximus are designed to make opportunity charging practical, which can reduce or eliminate the labor, space, and battery-room overhead of a swap program.
Can these chargers be installed outdoors?
Yes. Momentus and Maximus support both indoor and outdoor installation, wall-mounted or pedestal-mounted, so charging infrastructure can be placed at the dock, in a charging room, or in a covered outdoor area.
Do I need to change my forklift's charging connector?
Not necessarily. Momentus and Maximus support multiple connector options, including SB350, Euro, and REMA, so they can typically match the connectors already in use across a mixed-brand fleet.
How fast can Momentus or Maximus charge a forklift battery?
Momentus delivers up to 20 kW from a single port and Maximus delivers up to 40 kW across dual ports, both at up to 94% efficiency. Actual charge time depends on battery chemistry, pack size, and state of charge — a charging assessment will map this to your specific fleet.
