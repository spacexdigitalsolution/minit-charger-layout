What is the Minit Charger Mobilus?

Mobilus is a mobile DC fast charger with an onboard Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) — charger and generator in one — deployed on a trailer or skid platform to deliver charging power wherever it's needed, without a fixed electrical installation. It supports lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery chemistries with configurable connectors, and connects to AssetPro 360 / Cumulus with built-in GPS tracking for remote visibility.

Where fixed infrastructure runs out

Some charging needs can't wait on a utility timeline.

A temporary gate, a construction site, an emergency deployment — none of them come with a charger already installed. Here's where that gap shows up, and how Mobilus is built against it.

Failure mode

No fixed infrastructure where you need it

A temporary gate, a construction site, a disaster-response staging area — none of them have a charger already installed, and waiting on a utility interconnection isn't an option when equipment needs power today.

Design response

Mobilus brings DC fast charging to any site on a trailer or skid platform, delivering power wherever it's needed without a fixed installation.

Failure mode

A power outage strands an electric fleet

When grid power drops at a ramp or facility, chargers that depend entirely on that feed go down with it — right when backup power matters most.

Design response

Mobilus's onboard Battery Energy Storage System lets it function as a charger and generator in one, delivering clean power independent of a live grid connection.

Failure mode

Temporary demand doesn't justify permanent infrastructure

A construction project, a temporary terminal gate, a seasonal peak — building permanent charging infrastructure for a need that might not exist in six months rarely pencils out.

Design response

Mobilus relocates with the demand — deploy it where it's needed, then move it to the next site when the job is done.

Failure mode

Emergency response can't wait on a permitting timeline

A disaster-response deployment or an emergency backup power need doesn't come with the luxury of a permitting and interconnection schedule.

Design response

Mobilus is outdoor-rated and mobile by design, ready to deliver power the moment it arrives on site.

Failure mode

Mixed equipment shows up wherever you deploy

A mobile deployment often has to serve whatever equipment happens to be on site — different battery chemistries, different connectors — not a single predictable fleet.

Design response

Mobilus supports lead-acid, lithium, and EV chemistries with configurable, interchangeable connectors, adapting to whatever shows up.

Failure mode

No visibility into a charger that isn't on your property

Equipment deployed off-site or to a remote location is easy to lose track of — is it charging, is it idle, is it even still there?

Design response

Frequently asked questions

What buyers ask about Mobilus.

What is the Minit Charger Mobilus?
Mobilus is a mobile DC fast charger with an onboard Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) — charger and generator in one — deployed on a trailer or skid platform to deliver charging power wherever it's needed, without a fixed electrical installation.
How does Mobilus provide power without a fixed electrical connection?
Mobilus's onboard Battery Energy Storage System stores and delivers power independently of a live grid connection, so it can charge equipment at a site with no existing charging infrastructure and be recharged or relocated as needed.
What is Mobilus's power output?
Mobilus delivers 30–80 kW of DC fast charging with configurable port and connector options. Actual configuration depends on the deployment — a charging assessment will confirm the right setup.
What types of equipment can Mobilus charge?
Mobilus supports lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery chemistries with configurable, interchangeable connectors, so it can adapt to airport GSE, passenger EVs, transit vehicles, and other equipment on a mixed or temporary deployment.
Is Mobilus rated for outdoor and remote deployment?
Yes. Mobilus is outdoor-rated and built for mobile deployment on a trailer or skid platform, suited to construction sites, remote ramps, and emergency-response locations.
Can Mobilus be tracked once it's deployed off-site?
Yes. Mobilus includes built-in GPS tracking and connects to AssetPro 360 / Cumulus, so its location, charge status, and usage are visible remotely wherever it's deployed.
Does Mobilus integrate with AssetPro 360?
Yes. Mobilus is cloud-connected through AssetPro 360 / Cumulus for remote monitoring, charge history and analytics, and predictive maintenance alerts, the same as the rest of the Minit Charger fleet.
What situations is Mobilus best suited for?
Mobilus is built for temporary, remote, or emergency charging — construction sites, temporary airport gates, disaster-response deployments, and any location where fixed charging infrastructure doesn't exist yet or isn't practical to install.
