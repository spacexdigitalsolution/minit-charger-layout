What is the Minit Charger Momentus?

Momentus is a compact, single-port 20 kW DC fast charger built for opportunity charging of industrial electric vehicles — forklifts, pallet jacks, and other material handling equipment — in warehouses, distribution centers, and manufacturing facilities. It mounts to a wall or pedestal, supports lead-acid, lithium, EV, and AGM battery chemistries, and connects to AssetPro 360 / Cumulus for fleet-wide visibility.

Built for the everyday floor

Where a compact charger actually earns its keep.

A busy warehouse doesn't need the highest-power charger on the market — it needs one that installs fast, fits the floor plan, and keeps equipment moving. Here's where that shows up.

Failure mode

Battery swapping eats labor hours every shift

An operator pulled off the floor to swap a battery isn't picking, staging, or loading — and a spare-battery room adds its own labor and space overhead.

Design response

Momentus delivers fast opportunity charging between shifts and breaks, so equipment returns to operation sooner instead of waiting on a full swap cycle.

Failure mode

Chargers eat floor space you'd rather use for racking

A traditional floor-standing charger cabinet claims aisle space permanently, and doesn't move easily when the layout changes.

Design response

Modern SiC power electronics keep Momentus compact enough to wall-mount, so charging fits the floor plan instead of dictating it.

Failure mode

Mixed battery chemistries, one charger that can't keep up

Lead-acid forklifts, an AGM order picker, a lithium pilot program — most single-purpose chargers force a fleet to standardize before they'll support it.

Design response

Momentus charges lead-acid, lithium, EV, and AGM batteries from the same unit, so procurement isn't boxed in.

Failure mode

Electrical service doesn't match the charger

A facility running 208V service and another running 480V shouldn't need two different chargers just because the incoming voltage differs site to site.

Design response

Momentus switches between 208 and 480 VAC input with minimal effort, so the same charger works across sites with different electrical service.

Failure mode

No visibility into fleet uptime until a truck goes down

Without fleet-wide charge and battery-health data, the first sign of a problem is usually a forklift stalled mid-aisle.

Design response

CellTrac and AssetPro 360 track battery health, charge history, and predictive maintenance alerts, flagging a failing battery before it strands equipment.

Failure mode

Installation drags out the project timeline

A charger that needs a specialized crew or custom electrical work turns a simple equipment purchase into a multi-week installation project.

Design response

Momentus installs to a wall or pedestal with a single AC input connection — built for quick, straightforward installation.

Frequently asked questions

What buyers ask about Momentus.

What is the Minit Charger Momentus?
Momentus is a compact, single-port 20kW DC fast charger designed for opportunity charging of industrial electric vehicles — forklifts, pallet jacks, and other material handling equipment — in warehouses and distribution centers.
How much power does Momentus deliver?
Momentus delivers 20 kW from a single port at up to 94% efficiency. Actual charge time depends on battery chemistry, pack size, and state of charge.
Can Momentus be wall-mounted or does it need a pedestal?
Both are supported. Momentus installs to a wall to save floor space, or on a pedestal where a wall mount isn't an option — the same charger, two installation choices.
What batteries does Momentus support?
Momentus supports lead-acid, lithium, EV, and AGM battery chemistries from the same charger, with SB350, Euro, and REMA connector options.
Can Momentus run on both 208V and 480V power?
Yes. Momentus accepts a universal 208–480 VAC, 3-phase input and switches between 208V and 480V service with minimal effort, so the same charger works across sites with different electrical service.
How is Momentus different from Maximus?
Momentus is a compact single-port 20kW charger built for efficient opportunity charging where the higher power or dual-port throughput of Maximus isn't required. Maximus adds a second port and higher power output for higher-utilization, multi-shift operations.
Does Momentus integrate with AssetPro 360?
Yes. Momentus connects to AssetPro 360 / Cumulus and CellTrac for remote monitoring, battery health, charge history and analytics, and predictive maintenance alerts.
Is Momentus suitable for outdoor installation?
Yes, Momentus supports both indoor and outdoor installation, though it's most commonly deployed indoors in warehouses and distribution centers.
