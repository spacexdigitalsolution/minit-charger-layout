What is the Minit Charger Durus?

Durus is Minit Charger's most compact charger, built with integrated battery management and cloud connectivity for small electric equipment — golf carts, AGVs, and small industrial fleets. It can be permanently mounted onboard a single vehicle, or installed as a standalone unit that serves whichever vehicle needs it, with built-in GPS tracking and battery health monitoring feeding into AssetPro 360.

The smallest vehicles in a fleet often get the least charging attention — not because they matter less, but because most chargers weren't sized for them.

01

Small equipment doesn't get the same charging attention

A golf cart, a small AGV, a single utility cart — these don't justify a full pedestal charger, so they end up on a trickle charger or forgotten in a corner of the fleet plan.

Design responseDurus is purpose-built for exactly this scale of equipment, with the same cloud connectivity and battery management as the rest of the fleet, just sized to fit.

A mixed fleet needs a drawer full of chargers

Lead-acid, lithium, small EV packs — a mixed small-equipment fleet often means mismatched chargers, each with its own cable and quirks.

Set the right expectations

What actually affects your charging speed.

Charge time isn't just a function of the charger — the battery on the other end of the cable matters just as much. Here's what to factor in before comparing charge times across a fleet.

Battery capacity
A larger battery simply takes longer to fill — capacity and charge time scale together.
Charger output
Higher amps charge faster, within what the battery and Durus's 2.2–3.3kW range support.
Battery age & condition
Older or degraded batteries typically accept charge more slowly than a fresh pack.
Temperature
Extreme heat or cold affects charging efficiency — expect some variation outdoors in peak summer or winter.
Starting state of charge
Starting a session at 30–40% SOC charges faster than topping off from near-empty.

Frequently asked questions

What buyers ask about Durus.

What is the Minit Charger Durus?
Durus is Minit Charger's most compact charger, built with integrated battery management and cloud connectivity for small electric equipment — golf carts, AGVs, and small industrial fleets — either mounted onboard the vehicle or used as a standalone unit.
Can Durus be used as a standalone charger or only onboard a vehicle?
Both. Durus can be permanently mounted onboard a single vehicle, or installed as a standalone wall-mounted unit that serves whichever vehicle needs it — the same charger works either way.
What types of equipment is Durus designed for?
Durus is designed for small electric equipment such as golf carts, AGVs, and small industrial or utility fleets, and also applies to smaller airport GSE, material handling equipment, and warehouse applications.
What batteries does Durus support?
Durus supports lead-acid, lithium, and EV battery chemistries, with SB350, Euro, and open-lead connector options.
Does Durus track equipment location and battery health?
Yes. Durus includes built-in GPS tracking and battery health monitoring, feeding into the AssetPro 360 / Cumulus dashboard alongside the rest of the fleet.
Is Durus suitable for outdoor use?
Yes. Durus is rated from -25°C to 60°C and supports both indoor and outdoor installation.
How is Durus different from Momentus?
Momentus is a 20kW single-port charger for material handling equipment like forklifts. Durus is a much smaller-format 2.2–3.3kW charger built for compact equipment like golf carts and AGVs, and can mount onboard the vehicle itself rather than requiring a fixed station.
Does Durus integrate with AssetPro 360?
Yes. Durus connects to AssetPro 360 / Cumulus for remote monitoring, charge history, and predictive maintenance alerts, with battery health and GPS tracking built in.
What affects how fast Durus charges a battery?
Charge time depends on more than the charger itself: battery capacity, charger output (amps), battery age and condition, ambient temperature, and the battery's starting state of charge all affect how quickly a session completes. Starting a charge at 30–40% state of charge is typically faster than charging from near-empty, and extreme heat or cold can reduce charging efficiency.
